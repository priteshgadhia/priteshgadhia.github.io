Perspective

Technology is rarely the problem. Leadership, culture and conviction usually are.

Behaviour, not just operations

Every technology wave changes more than operations. It changes behaviour. Ecommerce created a new kind of buyer. Search made customers researchers and critics. Social media shifted trust from brands to peers. AI agents will increasingly compare, choose and decide on behalf of customers.

Adoption, not just adoption

The organisations that win are not the ones that simply adopt the technology. They are the ones that understand how behaviour is changing, redesign the experience around it and build teams that can absorb new ways of working.

Where it really happens

That is where transformation really happens: in the space between ambition and adoption, strategy and behaviour, technology and trust. It is the hardest part, and the part most programmes underestimate.
